3DPeek Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

3DPeek (“the app”) is made by Bradley Dalton. The app opens and displays 3D and CAD files entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your files

Files you open are read and rendered locally on your iPhone and are never uploaded. A list of recently opened files and your settings are stored only on your device. You can clear them in the app’s Settings, and deleting the app removes them.

Network use

The app may contact Expo’s update service (expo.dev) to download app updates. These requests contain technical information such as app version and platform, never your files or personal information. See Expo’s privacy policy. Links in the app open external websites, which have their own policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

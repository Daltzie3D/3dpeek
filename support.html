3DPeek Support

STEP & CAD File Viewer for iPhone · Updated 22 October 2026

Contact

Questions, bugs or a file that won’t open? Email daltzieai@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and (if you can share it) the file.

Supported formats

STEP/STP, IGES/IGS, BREP, FreeCAD (FCStd), STL, OBJ, 3MF, glTF/GLB, FBX, 3DS, AMF, Rhino 3DM, IFC, dotbim (BIM), COLLADA (DAE), OFF, PLY, VRML (WRL).

Opening files

  • In the app, tap Open and pick a file from Files, iCloud Drive or another provider.
  • Recently opened files appear on the home screen; clear them in Settings.

Tips

  • Very large CAD files (especially STEP and IFC) can take a while and use a lot of memory. Close other apps if loading fails.
  • Everything runs on your device, so no internet connection is needed to view models.

Privacy

3DPeek collects no data. Read the privacy policy.

Open source

3DPeek uses three.js, Open CASCADE (via occt-import-js, LGPL-2.1), rhino3dm and web-ifc. Licences are listed in the app. Viewer source code: https://github.com/Daltzie3D/3dpeek-viewer.
